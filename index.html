欢迎使用「大老二与唐诗」（以下简称「本游戏」）。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本游戏会处理哪些信息、如何使用与保护这些信息，以及您享有的权利。

一、我们处理的信息

1.1 仅存储在您设备上的数据

本游戏的以下数据仅保存在您的设备本地（应用私有存储），不会上传到我们的服务器，卸载应用即被删除：

  • 游戏进度、得分、金币与成就
  • 玩家昵称、语言、音乐与音量等设置

1.2 广告相关数据（由 Google AdMob 处理）

本游戏通过 Google AdMob 展示插屏广告以支持免费运营。为提供广告服务，Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能收集并处理：

  • 设备广告标识符（Advertising ID / 广告 ID）
  • 设备型号、操作系统版本、屏幕与语言等信息
  • 网络连接信息（IP 地址、网络类型）
  • 广告展示、点击与转化数据
  • 基于 IP 地址推断的大致地理位置

上述信息用于投放个性化或非个性化广告、衡量广告效果以及防范欺诈与无效流量。关于 Google 如何使用这些数据，请参阅：

  • Google 如何使用使用我们合作伙伴服务的网站或应用中的信息
  • Google 隐私权政策
  • AdMob 计划政策

1.3 我们不会收集的信息

本游戏不会主动收集您的姓名、电话号码、电子邮件、通讯录、照片、精确位置等个人身份信息。您在游戏内输入的昵称仅用于游戏内显示，不与任何个人身份信息关联。

二、信息的使用目的

  • 提供游戏功能，保存并恢复您的游戏进度与设置
  • 通过 Google AdMob 展示广告并衡量广告效果，以维持游戏免费运营
  • 排查崩溃与性能问题，提升稳定性

三、信息的存储、保留与安全

  • 游戏数据仅存储于您的设备本地，卸载应用或清除应用数据后即被删除
  • 广告相关数据由 Google 按其隐私政策规定的期限保留与处理，我们不在自有服务器上存储这些数据
  • 我们采取合理的安全措施保护本地数据，但无法保证绝对安全

四、广告与您的选择

4.1 个性化广告

默认情况下，广告可能基于您的广告 ID 进行个性化投放。您可以随时退出个性化广告：

  • Android 设备：设置 → 隐私（或「安全与隐私」）→ 广告 → 选择「删除广告 ID」或关闭「个性化广告」
  • 重置广告 ID 后，此前基于该 ID 的广告偏好将不再与之关联

4.2 广告偏好管理

您可访问 Google 广告设置 管理 Google 广告的个性化偏好。

五、欧洲经济区与英国用户（GDPR）

若您位于欧洲经济区（EEA）或英国，依据《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）及英国相关法律，您享有以下权利：

  • 知情权：了解我们如何处理您的数据
  • 访问与更正权：访问或更正与您相关的数据
  • 删除权：请求删除与您相关的数据
  • 限制处理与反对权：反对基于合法权益的处理，或限制处理
  • 撤回同意：随时撤回对个性化广告的同意；首次启动时，本游戏会通过 Google 认证的同意管理平台（UMP）征求您的同意，您也可在广告内或设备设置中更改选择
  • 投诉权：向您所在国家或地区的数据保护机构投诉

行使上述权利请发送邮件至 battery360@163.com，我们将在 30 天内回复。

六、儿童隐私

本游戏面向 13 岁及以上用户，不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。若您是家长或监护人，发现孩子向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快删除。

七、第三方服务

本游戏集成了 Google AdMob（Google LLC）用于展示广告。该服务的数据处理行为受 Google 隐私政策约束。除广告服务外，本游戏不集成其他会收集个人数据的第三方 SDK，也不包含账号系统、支付或社交分享功能。

八、政策变更

我们可能不定期更新本政策。更新后的版本将在本页面发布，并同步更新上方的日期。若变更涉及您权利的重大调整，我们会在应用内提示。建议您定期查阅。

Welcome to "Big Two with Tang Poetry" (the "Game"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the Game processes, how it is used and protected, and the rights you have.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Data stored only on your device

The following data is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Game progress, scores, coins and achievements
  • Player nickname, language, music and volume settings

1.2 Advertising data (processed by Google AdMob)

The Game displays interstitial ads through Google AdMob to remain free. To provide advertising services, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device model, OS version, screen and language information
  • Network connection information (IP address, network type)
  • Ad impression, click and conversion data
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address

This information is used to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and invalid traffic. For how Google uses this data, please see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Program Policies

1.3 Information we do not collect

The Game does not actively collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Nicknames you enter are used only for in-game display and are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To provide game features and save/restore your progress and settings
  • To display and measure ads through Google AdMob so the Game can remain free
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance

3. Storage, Retention and Security

  • Game data is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data
  • Advertising data is retained and processed by Google in accordance with its privacy policy; we do not store such data on our own servers
  • We apply reasonable security measures to local data but cannot guarantee absolute security

4. Ads and Your Choices

4.1 Personalized advertising

By default, ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. You can opt out at any time:

  • On Android: Settings → Privacy (or Security & Privacy) → Ads, then "Delete advertising ID" or turn off ad personalization
  • After resetting your Advertising ID, previous ad preferences linked to it are no longer associated with you

4.2 Ad preference management

You can manage Google ad personalization preferences at Google Ads Settings.

5. EEA and UK Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR and applicable UK law:

  • Right to be informed about how we process your data
  • Access and rectification: access or correct data relating to you
  • Erasure: request deletion of data relating to you
  • Restriction and objection: restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent to personalized ads at any time; on first launch the Game requests consent through Google's certified consent management platform (UMP), and you may change your choice in the ad or in device settings
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email battery360@163.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display ads. That service's data processing is governed by Google's privacy policy. Apart from advertising, the Game integrates no other third-party SDKs that collect personal data, and includes no account system, payments, or social sharing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date above. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you in the app. Please review this page periodically.
